Welcome to my website! I'm a Data Scientist at the [Allen Institute for Cell Science](https://alleninstitute.org/) in Seattle working on representation learning for cell biology. Our work focuses on uncovering the design principles of how cells are organized and function, leveraging deep learning and computer vision to build interpretable representations of 3D cellular structures. My primary areas of interest lie in Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Computational Biology.

In 2024, I graduated with a Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Washington, Seattle. During my time at UW, I was involved in a joint research project with the [BioNLP Lab](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~swang/) and the [GRAIL Lab](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/), developing robust representations for OCT-IR imaging data. I was primarily advised by [Prof. Linda Shapiro](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~shapiro/) and worked on multimodal and contrastive learning for large histopathology datasets and implementing NeRFs for custom datasets. During the summer and fall of 2023, I interned at [Alpenglow Biosciences](https://www.alpenglowbiosciences.com/), solving the neural style transfer problem for histopathology.

I also briefly worked at the [Ubiquitous Computing Lab](https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/), focusing on the noninvasive measurement of vital signals using MMWave Radar for physiological sensing.
<br><br>
I completed my Undergraduate Thesis (2022) focused on optimizing adversarial learning at [Spectrum Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/spectrumlabeeiisc/spectrum-lab?authuser=0), Indian Institute of Science with [Prof. Chandra Sekhar Seelamantula](https://ee.iisc.ac.in/chandra-sekhar-seelamantula/). During my undergraduate studies, I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Jora Gonda](https://eee.nitk.ac.in/professor/JMG) and [Prof. Krishnan CMC](https://sites.google.com/view/krishnan-chemmangat).

In 2022, I graduated as salutatorian from the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, India, with a Bachelor's in Electrical Engineering and a minor in Computer Science.
